Meet Yaz

Raised on the Southwest Side. Built for this.

Daughter of Mexican immigrants. Raised in Gage Park, Chicago. I grew up watching my parents pour everything into giving us a better life — and I learned early that our communities are full of power, creativity, and innovation that the world doesn't always reflect back to us.

That's why I built a wellness practice that goes beyond the gym and the meal plan. I work with high-achievers — especially first-gen folks — who are doing all the things but quietly running on empty. My job is to help you find your way back to yourself.

The Framework

Four pillars. One whole life.

Wellness isn't just what you eat or how you move. It's how you think, how you connect, what you believe in, and how you show up for the world.

Mind

Clarity, creativity, and the confidence to take the next step anyway. A healthy mind doesn't mean having it all figured out — it means trusting yourself enough to move forward. You'll figure it out.

Clarity Creativity Growth Intention

Body

Your body is not a project to optimize. It's a home to honor. Strength, breath, rest, and pleasure — all of it counts. Count colors, not calories.

Strength Breath Recovery Pleasure

Spirit

The thread connecting you to everything larger than yourself — ancestors, earth, plants, animals, your inner child, your future self. Encomendándote a Dios.

Ancestry Prayer Nature Stillness

Community

Wellness doesn't happen in isolation. Your neighborhood, your relationships, the sociopolitical world around you — all of it is part of your health. Find your role in the pursuit of justice and belonging.

Belonging Local Roots Action Connection
